## Confidence And Lock Tuning

Ran tuning sweeps on representative official 1600x900 sequences: diagonal motion, speed variation, and reacquisition.

Best balanced config:

```yaml
classifier.confidence_threshold: 0.25
temporal.min_confirmations: 2
tracker.association_gate_px: 120
```

Full tuned evaluation output: `outputs/unity-evaluation/official_1600x900_v2_tuned_conf025`

- Candidate recall: 0.999650
- Accepted recall: 0.245962
- Filtered MAE: 6.288764 px
- Locked frames: 50.805556%
- CPU FPS: 7.981692

This improves accepted recall and lock percentage while keeping localization error low. More aggressive thresholds increased lock on weak sequences but caused large pixel error, so they were not adopted.
